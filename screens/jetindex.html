Jet Index

Real-time Airfare Price Index for India's Consumer Price Index. Automated pipeline that scrapes online fares, unbundles fare components, and computes a DGCA-weighted Laspeyres price index across 20 domestic routes - built for Smart India Hackathon 2026.

Problem & solution

! Problem

India's CPI airfare data is collected manually at airport counters

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) collects airfare data manually at airport counters - covering only ~30% of bookings and missing daily price swings of 200-400%. This incomplete data feeds directly into India's Consumer Price Index, affecting policy decisions for 1.4 billion people. The manual process is slow, error-prone, and structurally incapable of capturing real-time fare dynamics.

→ Solution

Automated scraping pipeline with index computation

JetIndex scrapes fare quotes from IndiGo and MakeMyTrip nightly across 20 DGCA-weighted routes and 5 lead times (200 scrape jobs). Each quote is unbundled into base fare, UDF, taxes, convenience fee, and other fees for component-level tracking. Computes 8 index formulas (Laspeyres primary) with a 30+ day backtest validated against DGCA monthly averages. 50+ API endpoints, interactive dashboard with trend charts and route heatmaps.
